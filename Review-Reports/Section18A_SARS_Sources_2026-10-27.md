# SARS Section 18A Sources — 27 October 2026
> **ASCO status:** CIPC-registered NPC 2026/521895/08 | SARS PBO Exemption Reference **930092287** | Section 18A approval effective **9 October 2026**.
>


- SARS Section 18A and IT3(d) Third Party Data: https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/tax-exempt-institutions/section-18a-and-it3d-third-party-data/
  - Approved Section 18A entities must submit IT3(d) third-party data.
  - Current submission periods stated by SARS: bi-annual due 31 October for 1 March–31 August; annual due 31 May for 1 March–last day of February.
  - SARS notes that Public Notice 6762 applies from 1 March 2026 and adds required receipt information.
- SARS Basic Guide to Section 18A Approval, Issue 5: https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/Legal-Pub-Guide-IT17-Basic-Guide-to-Section-18A-Approval.pdf
  - Receipt must include the Section 18A reference number, donation received date, organisation name/address, donor name/address, cash amount or nature/value of non-cash donation, and certification of exclusive use for the relevant public benefit activity.
  - Additional fields include donor nature, natural-person identification type and country, juristic-person identification/registration number, trade name where different, donor income-tax reference, contact number, email and unique receipt number.
  - Donated services such as time, skill or effort do not qualify as a donation of property in kind.
- SARS Public Benefit Organisations: https://www.sars.gov.za/businesses-and-employers/tax-exempt-institutions/public-benefit-organisations/
  - Section 18A approval is distinct from PBO approval and is restricted to specific approved organisations and approved public benefit activities.
- SARS Public Notice 6762, Government Gazette 53589, effective 1 March 2026: https://www.sars.gov.za/legal-lsec-it-gn-2025-04-notice-6762-gg-53589-notice-ito-s-18a2avii-prescribing-further-info-contained-in-receipt-ito-s-18a2avii-24-october-2025/
  - Mandatory additional donor information: nature of person; natural-person ID type and country; juristic-person ID/registration number; trade name where different; income-tax reference; contact number; email; in-kind property description and deemed deduction amount where applicable; unique receipt number.
