# ASCO Section 18A Document Update Report — 27 October 2026

## Verified SARS approval

The authoritative SARS letter dated 26 October 2026 confirms that African Solidarity Civic Organization is an approved Public Benefit Organisation under Exemption Reference **930092287** and is approved to issue tax-deductible receipts under section 18A(1)(a)(i) effective **9 October 2026**. The SARS taxpayer reference is **9322812265**. The PBO exemption effective date is **15 September 2026**.

The approved Section 18A activities are limited to the promotion or advocacy of human rights and democracy, and the promotion or protection of family stability. ASCO must not issue Section 18A receipts for every programme or sponsorship automatically. Each receipt must be linked to a qualifying donation used solely for an approved Section 18A activity.

## Documents changed

ASCO-authored operational, donor-compliance, governance-support, privacy and strategic documents were backed up under `Historical Records/Pre-Section18A-2026-10-26/`, updated with the verified status and dates, and regenerated to PDF where a corresponding PDF existed. Outdated statements that Section 18A was pending or that ASCO was not authorised to issue receipts were removed from current operational documents.

The MOI, Constitution, MOI Board Minutes, PBO Motivation, SARS submission records and other historical/legal records were preserved rather than rewritten. The original SARS approval letter was copied unchanged to `Section-18A/SARS_Section_18A_Approval_Letter_2026-10-26.pdf`.

## Receipt suite

The new Section 18A receipt suite contains an editable DOCX master, a print-ready PDF, an interactive fillable PDF and an internal issuing guide. The template includes the organisation and donor fields required by section 18A and SARS Public Notice 6762 effective 1 March 2026, including donor nature, identification or registration information, income tax reference, contact details, email, in-kind donation description and deemed amount where applicable, and a unique receipt number.

The recommended internal numbering convention is `ASCO-S18A-YYYY-NNNN`. This is an ASCO control convention, not a SARS-prescribed number format. ASCO should maintain a controlled register of issued, voided and corrected receipts and submit required IT3(d) third-party data.

## Items requiring operational review

Before issuing a receipt, ASCO should confirm that the donation is a bona fide qualifying donation, that it is allocated solely to one of the two approved Section 18A activities, that any property-in-kind valuation is defensible, and that the authorised representative signs the completed receipt. Donated services such as time, skill or effort should not be treated as qualifying property in kind.
